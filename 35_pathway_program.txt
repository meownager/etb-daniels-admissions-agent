PATHWAY PROGRAM — DIRECT UNDERGRADUATE TO MASTER'S TRACK

The Daniels School Pathway Program is a streamlined admission track that allows current undergraduate students and recent graduates to apply to a master's program with reduced application requirements. It is available for the following seven programs: MS Accounting, MS Business Analytics and Information Management, Master of Business and Technology, MS Finance, MS Global Supply Chain Management, MS Human Resource Management, and MS Marketing.

The Pathway Program was initially developed for the MS Accounting program because CPA certification requires the same number of credit hours as a master's degree, making the MSA a natural continuation of undergraduate accounting study. The program was subsequently expanded to the other six residential master's programs based on the same logic: motivated undergraduate students benefit from staying connected to an institution where they have already built relationships, familiar campus resources, and established study habits.

Eligible applicants include current Purdue West Lafayette undergraduates and students from select partner schools. The key benefit of the Pathway Program is the reduction of application materials — several components that are required for standard applicants are waived, including the TOEFL and IELTS for U.S. graduates. This makes the transition administratively simpler and allows students to spend less time on paperwork and more time on coursework and career preparation.

Students considering the Pathway Program are encouraged to begin planning as early as their sophomore year. This allows enough time to take any prerequisite coursework, connect with program advisors, and ensure the academic record supports the application. The program does not require exceptional grades — it requires a genuine plan and early engagement.

For a current Purdue undergraduate asking about graduate school options: the Pathway Program is typically the most efficient and lowest-friction route to a Daniels master's degree. For a student from another institution, it is worth asking whether their school is a Pathway partner — contacting businessgradadmissions@purdue.edu is the fastest way to confirm.
