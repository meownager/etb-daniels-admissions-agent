CAREER GOAL — DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION, TECHNOLOGY LEADERSHIP, OR LEADING AI AND DATA STRATEGY

Primary recommendation: Master of Business and Technology (MBT).

The MBT is designed specifically for the professional who wants to lead at the intersection of technology and business strategy — not just participate in technology projects, but drive them. The curriculum includes computational business intelligence, strategic management of technology, and how emerging technologies reshape business models. It is available in both residential and online formats, with an Indianapolis Experience option for the online version.

Key framing: digital transformation and AI strategy roles require someone who understands both the business case and the technology well enough to lead teams that include engineers and data scientists. The MBT builds exactly that profile. This is not a technical degree — students do not write code. They learn how to evaluate technology decisions, set strategy, and lead technology-enabled business models.
