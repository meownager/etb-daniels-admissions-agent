MASTER OF BUSINESS AND TECHNOLOGY (MBT) — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The MBT is a one-year program available in residential and online formats. It is one of the more selective programs at Daniels in terms of test score preferences, reflecting the technical and business rigor of the curriculum.

The preferred GMAT score for the MBT is 655 — higher than the preferred score for any other Daniels master's program. However, waiver conditions are meaningful. Applicants with a STEM degree from a recognized institution and a GPA of 3.3 or higher may waive the GMAT without additional requirements. Applicants with two or more years of professional experience and a STEM undergraduate degree with a GPA of 3.0 or higher also qualify for a waiver. Applicants without a STEM degree are expected to submit GMAT or GRE scores. GRE scores are accepted in lieu of GMAT for applicants who have already taken the GRE. GMAT is preferred for scholarship consideration. Waiver inquiries should be directed to the admissions office after beginning the application.

Work experience is preferred but not required for the residential program. The program attracts both recent graduates with strong STEM backgrounds and professionals with several years of experience in engineering, technology, or operations who want to add business leadership skills.

Deadlines are rolling for the MBT. Applying earlier in the cycle is strongly recommended for scholarship consideration and for securing a cohort spot, as the program has a smaller cohort than the broader MBA programs.

For an applicant with an engineering or computer science background who is worried about the GMAT: if they have a STEM degree with a GPA of 3.3 or above, or two or more years of experience with a STEM 3.0 GPA, they qualify for a waiver. The GMAT score of 655 is a preference for applicants who cannot waive, not a floor for everyone. The admissions committee reviews the full application, and a strong engineering record with clear business leadership goals is competitive for this program.
