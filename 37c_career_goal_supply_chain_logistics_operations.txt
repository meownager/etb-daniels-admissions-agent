CAREER GOAL — SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT, LOGISTICS, PROCUREMENT, OPERATIONS, OR DEMAND PLANNING

Primary recommendation: MS Global Supply Chain Management (MSGSCM).

The MSGSCM offers the highest earning ceiling of any Daniels MS program, with the highest reported salary reaching $180,000. Supply chain roles are projected to grow 19 percent through 2033. The program has a direct recruiting pipeline to FedEx, GM, P&G, Cummins, and Carrier, and Purdue's engineering heritage gives it genuine technical depth that pure business schools cannot match.

Secondary recommendation: One-Year MBA for professionals with 5 or more years in supply chain who want strategic leadership skills beyond functional expertise, or for those targeting VP or director-level cross-functional leadership rather than supply chain operations specifically.

Key framing: the MSGSCM is the right choice when the goal is to build deep expertise and advance within the supply chain field. The MBA is the right choice when the goal is to use supply chain experience as a platform for broader business leadership.
