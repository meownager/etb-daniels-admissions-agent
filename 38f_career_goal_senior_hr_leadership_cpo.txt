CAREER GOAL — CHIEF PEOPLE OFFICER PATH OR SENIOR HR LEADERSHIP

Primary recommendation: MSHRM for candidates who want the formal credential and access to organizational development and leadership coaching curriculum.

Secondary recommendation: One-Year MBA for candidates with significant HR experience who want broader business credentials alongside people strategy expertise — useful for the CPO path at companies where the role reports to the CEO and requires cross-functional business fluency.

Key framing: the distinction is whether the candidate wants to deepen their HR expertise with a formal credential (MSHRM) or wants to expand beyond HR into broader business leadership while retaining people strategy as their core (One-Year MBA). Both are legitimate paths to senior HR leadership — the right choice depends on where the candidate currently has gaps.
