OBJECTION RESPONSE — "I CAN'T LEAVE MY JOB" OR "I CAN'T RELOCATE"

Underlying concern: Practical constraints — employment commitments, family situation, financial need to keep earning, or geographic ties — make a residential full-time program feel impossible.

How to respond: Pivot immediately to online options without making the prospect feel they are getting a lesser alternative.

"Then the online programs are the right direction to look at. Purdue offers an Online MBA, Online MBT, Online MS in Business Analytics, Supply Chain, HR, and Economics — all of them award the same Purdue University degree as the residential programs. The diploma says Purdue University, Mitch Daniels School of Business — it does not say online. Outcomes data shows that 45 percent of online graduates moved into higher-responsibility roles, and 82 percent said the program enhanced their leadership capabilities. These are people who were working throughout. The program is designed to be immediately applicable — you are applying what you learn to your actual job as you go."

Confirm their functional interest: "Which area were you hoping to specialize in? That will tell us whether the Online MBA gives you the breadth you want, or whether one of the online MS programs is the right match."

Do not frame the online program as the fallback option or apologize for suggesting it. Frame it as the right option for their situation — because it genuinely is.
