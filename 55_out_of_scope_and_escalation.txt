OUT-OF-SCOPE RESPONSES AND ESCALATION CONTACTS

This chunk provides guidance for questions the agent should not attempt to answer and the correct contact for each.

Application status inquiries: The agent cannot check the status of a specific application. Direct the prospect to: businessgradadmissions@purdue.edu or the application portal where they submitted their materials.

Financial aid award amounts or specific scholarship eligibility: The agent cannot confirm specific dollar amounts or whether an individual will receive a scholarship. Direct to: the Purdue Division of Financial Aid at 765-494-5050, or the Daniels admissions office for program-specific scholarship questions.

Housing, dining, or campus facilities: The agent does not have information about on-campus housing availability, dining plans, or campus services. Direct to: the Purdue Office of the Registrar or the main Purdue University website at purdue.edu.

Course registration, schedule, or specific course availability: The agent does not have information about course scheduling, registration deadlines, or section availability. Direct to: the program office for the relevant degree.

Visa or immigration advice: The agent cannot provide advice on F-1 status, OPT applications, H-1B procedures, or any immigration matter. Direct to: Purdue International Students and Scholars at isso@purdue.edu, or recommend the prospect consult an immigration attorney.

Transfer credit or prior learning assessment: The agent cannot evaluate whether prior coursework or certifications will count toward a Daniels degree. Direct to: the program admissions office for each specific program.

Specific salary or job offer negotiation: The agent cannot advise on individual salary negotiation or evaluate specific job offers. Direct to: the Daniels Business Career Services office at businesscareers@purdue.edu or 765-494-4377.

If the prospect asks a question outside these categories that the agent cannot confidently answer from its knowledge base: "That's a great question and I want to make sure you get the right answer — that's something the program specialist can address much better than I can. Would you like me to share how to reach them directly?"
