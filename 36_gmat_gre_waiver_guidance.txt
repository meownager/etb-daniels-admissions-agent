GMAT AND GRE WAIVER GUIDANCE

Many prospective students worry about standardized tests before they need to. The reality is that for most Daniels School programs, a GMAT or GRE waiver is available under conditions that many applicants already meet.

The most common waiver condition is GPA. For the MS Accounting, MSBAIM, and several other specialized master's programs, applicants with a cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0 or higher do not need to submit a GMAT or GRE. The test scores are encouraged for scholarship consideration and to make a competitive application stronger, but they are not required for admission eligibility.

A STEM degree is another frequent waiver pathway. For the MS Finance, MSGSCM, MBT, and One-Year MBA, applicants who hold a STEM undergraduate degree with a qualifying GPA can waive the GMAT. The specific GPA threshold varies by program — 3.0, 3.3, or higher depending on the program — so confirming with the admissions office for each specific program is important.

Work experience is the waiver pathway for online programs. For the Online MBA and Online MS Business Analytics, applicants with three or more years of professional work experience have the GMAT automatically waived without needing to apply for a waiver. For other online programs like MSGSCM and MSHRM, work experience reduces the emphasis on test scores even where scores are not explicitly required.

Professional certifications can also support a waiver case. For the MSF, holding a CFA Level II designation, a CPA certification, or equivalent professional credential is recognized as evidence of quantitative competence and supports a waiver application.

For applicants who do not meet a standard waiver condition, a waiver application is often still available. The process typically involves contacting businessgradadmissions@purdue.edu after starting an application. The admissions committee considers the full application holistically — strong recommendations, a compelling Statement of Purpose, and relevant professional experience can support a waiver even outside the standard conditions.

The admissions process at Daniels is described by the associate director of admissions as holistic. Test scores play one part. The program cares about career goals, professional potential, and the quality of the application as a whole.
