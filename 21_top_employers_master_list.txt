TOP EMPLOYERS HIRING DANIELS SCHOOL GRADUATES — 2024–2025

The following companies hired graduates from Daniels School master's programs in the most recent employment cycle. Numbers in parentheses indicate confirmed hires where more than one graduate was placed.

In accounting and finance, Capital One hired 13 graduates — the single largest employer across all programs. EY hired 6, Forvis Mazars hired 6, Deloitte hired 5, Crowe hired 5, KSM hired 5, KPMG hired 4, Equity Methods hired 4, and PwC hired 4. Other accounting and finance employers include BDO, Blue and Co., Deutsche Bank, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan Chase, Raymond James Financial, Truist, Santander Consumer, Cerity Partners, StoneX, TIAA, and Ringold Financial Management.

In technology and analytics, Amazon hired 9 graduates, IBM, Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, Western Digital, ASML, Micron, Tesla, and TeamViewer each hired graduates, and TSMC and Foxconn each hired multiple. Other technology employers include Genpact, Publicis Sapient, Optomi, Syniti, and TribolaTech.

In supply chain and manufacturing, FedEx hired 6 graduates, OMP hired 4, Foxconn hired 3, CNH Industrial hired 2, Belden hired 2, and Nestle hired 2. Single hires came from Cummins, Carrier, P&G, GM, The Andersons, Wintec Industries, Yazaki, Dometic, Arconic, Polaris, and Lennox.

In consulting, ZS hired 2 graduates, and other consulting employers include Efficio Consulting, Publicis Sapient, Tiger Analytics, Russell Reynolds Associates, Deloitte India, and Genpact.

In pharmaceutical and life sciences, employers include Eli Lilly, Bristol Myers Squibb, Genentech, Rigel Pharmaceuticals, Pharmerica, and Alto Pharmacy.

In consumer goods and retail, employers include P&G, PepsiCo with 3 hires, Mondelez International, Nestle with 2 hires, Mars, SharkNinja, Williams Sonoma, AutoZone, Home Depot, Wayfair, Sherwin-Williams, and CarMax.

In financial technology, employers include SoFi, Rocket Mortgage, Credit Karma, Remitly, GreenSky, and Forter.

In defense and aerospace, employers include L3Harris Technologies, Thales, IMMI, and Carrier.

This employer list reflects graduate-level hiring. Undergraduate employers are overlapping but include additional regional employers and a broader range of entry-level positions across all business functions.
