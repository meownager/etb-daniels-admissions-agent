ONLINE MBA — CAREER OUTCOMES

The Online MBA from the Daniels School of Business is a flexible program for working professionals who want a full MBA credential without interrupting their career. The program delivers the same curriculum and awards the same Purdue University degree as the residential One-Year MBA. The diploma does not indicate online — it says Purdue University, Mitch Daniels School of Business, Master of Business Administration.

Purdue's online MBA program is ranked among the best online MBA programs in the United States by U.S. News and World Report for 2025. The program is 48 credit hours and can typically be completed in approximately two years while working full time, with flexibility to pace faster or slower depending on the student's schedule.

Outcomes data for online program graduates is compelling. 82 percent reported that their managerial and leadership capabilities were enhanced by the program. 45 percent moved into roles with higher responsibilities, either at their current company or a new one. 88 percent reported that their degree increased their ability to work outside their functional area to solve business challenges. 77 percent were able to implement meaningful changes in their organizations.

The framing that resonates most with working professionals is that the Online MBA is designed to be immediately applicable. Students are applying what they learn to decisions they are making in real time, not studying case studies from a decade ago in a classroom disconnected from their actual work. The peer cohort in the online program includes professionals with significant experience, many of them at the ten-year-plus level, which elevates the quality of discussion and the value of the network.

Career outcomes vary by incoming background and goals. The online MBA serves three distinct types of students: those who want to advance in their current company, those who want to move to a new company at a higher level, and those who want to shift industries while continuing to work. All three trajectories are well-supported by the program's career services and alumni network.

For a prospect who says they cannot leave their job or cannot relocate, the Online MBA should be the first option discussed. Confirm which area they want to focus in, then assess whether the broader MBA or a specific online MS program is the better match.
