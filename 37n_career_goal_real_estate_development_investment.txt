CAREER GOAL — REAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT, COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE, OR REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT

Primary recommendation: MS Finance (MSF).

The MSF is the strongest fit for real estate investment, real estate private equity, REIT analysis, and commercial real estate finance roles. The program's financial analysis foundation — valuation, capital structure, investment analysis — maps directly to the analytical work that drives real estate investment decisions.

Secondary recommendation: One-Year MBA for applicants targeting real estate development, brokerage leadership, or general management roles within real estate operating companies. The MBA's breadth serves a developer or operator role better than the MSF's finance specialization.

Key framing: Daniels does not offer a dedicated real estate concentration — the real estate career path is built through coursework choice, internships, and networking rather than a specialized track. For applicants who require a dedicated real estate program (Masters of Real Estate Development, MRED), be honest that a specialized program may fit better, and recommend they discuss fit with the admissions office.

For applicants coming from real estate sales or brokerage (residential or commercial) who want to move into the investment side, the MSF is the clearest path. For applicants coming from construction, architecture, or development who want to move into the capital and investment side of the business, either MSF or One-Year MBA can work depending on whether the target role is analyst-track (MSF) or general management (MBA).

Top employers who recruit from Daniels include financial services firms that have significant real estate practices — Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan — and regional commercial real estate employers in the Midwest.
