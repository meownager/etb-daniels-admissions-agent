MS BUSINESS ANALYTICS AND INFORMATION MANAGEMENT (MSBAIM) — CAREER OUTCOMES

The MSBAIM is a one-year residential program at the Daniels School designed for students with a quantitative or technical background who want to build careers in business analytics, data science, business intelligence, or information systems management. The program sits deliberately at the intersection of business strategy and data technology — graduates are not pure data scientists or pure business generalists, but professionals who can translate complex data into business decisions.

Employment outcomes for MSBAIM graduates are the strongest of any specialized master's program at Daniels. 96 percent of graduates find full-time employment within six months, the highest rate of any program. The average full-time starting salary is $104,779, and the highest reported salary in the most recent cycle was $160,000. Job growth in business analytics and related roles is projected at 25 percent through 2030, making this one of the most favorable labor markets of any graduate business field.

Typical job titles for MSBAIM graduates include Business Analyst, Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Business Intelligence Analyst, Product Analyst, IT Consultant, and Systems Analyst. The range of employers is broad. Amazon hired 9 Daniels graduates in the most recent cycle, Capital One hired 13, and other employers include IBM, Cisco, TSMC, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Palo Alto Networks, ZS, Tiger Analytics, and Publicis Sapient. Graduates work across technology, financial services, consulting, manufacturing, retail, and healthcare.

A key distinction to communicate when recommending this program: MSBAIM graduates are not positioned as data scientists who write code all day. They are positioned as the professionals who sit between the data team and the leadership team — people who understand the technical side well enough to work with data engineers, and who understand the business side well enough to present findings to executives and drive decisions. This profile is increasingly what large employers want, and it is exactly what this program builds.

For a prospect with a quantitative undergraduate background — STEM, economics, computer science, mathematics, statistics, or business analytics — who wants a career where analytical skills open strategic doors, the MSBAIM is consistently the strongest recommendation at Daniels.
