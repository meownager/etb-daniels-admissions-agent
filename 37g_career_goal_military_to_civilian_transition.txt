CAREER GOAL — MILITARY TO CIVILIAN CAREER TRANSITION

Primary recommendation: One-Year MBA.

The One-Year MBA has a strong track record with military professionals. The STEM designation is advantageous for this group, and the breadth of the curriculum directly addresses the challenge of translating military leadership experience into corporate management roles. Several alumni have gone directly into management consulting and operations leadership from this background.

Key framing: military professionals bring leadership experience, discipline, and operational thinking that is genuinely valued in the MBA cohort and by employers. The MBA gives them the business vocabulary and functional credentials that make that experience legible to civilian employers who haven't seen it directly. The 11-month format means the transition from military service to civilian career is not drawn out.
