MS MARKETING (MSM) — CAREER OUTCOMES

The MS Marketing program at the Daniels School is a one-year residential program designed for students and professionals targeting careers in brand management, digital marketing, consumer insights, and marketing analytics. What distinguishes this program from a standard marketing degree is the integration of analytics — graduates are not just creative strategists but professionals who can interpret consumer behavior data and justify marketing investment with evidence. This combination is increasingly what employers require.

Employment outcomes show 78 percent of MSM graduates finding full-time positions within six months of graduation. The average starting salary is $81,600, and the highest reported salary in the most recent cycle reached $120,000.

Typical job titles include Brand Manager, Marketing Analyst, Digital Marketing Specialist, Consumer Insights Analyst, Marketing Strategist, Account Manager, and Product Marketing Manager. The employer relationships for this program are particularly strong in consumer goods. Top employers include P&G, PepsiCo with 3 hires, Mondelez International, Mars, SharkNinja, Williams Sonoma, Sherwin-Williams, Annalect, and Publicis Sapient. Graduates work across consumer goods, technology, retail, healthcare, and advertising and media industries.

Purdue's geographic positioning gives this program a specific advantage for consumer goods careers. P&G, PepsiCo, and Mars all have deep recruiting relationships with the school, and students who want to enter the CPG sector — one of the most sought-after marketing tracks — have a genuine pipeline advantage through this program.

The Pathway Program is available for MSM, allowing Purdue undergraduates and select partner school graduates to transition directly from undergraduate study into the master's program with reduced application requirements.

For a prospect with a marketing undergraduate background who wants more depth, or for an early professional in a marketing or sales role who wants to add analytical skills and move into a brand strategy position, the MSM is the direct recommendation. For someone who wants broader business skills alongside marketing, the One-Year MBA with marketing electives may be a better fit — a conversation about their specific goals will clarify which direction makes more sense.
