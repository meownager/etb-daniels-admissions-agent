TUITION AND COST OVERVIEW

The following tuition figures are approximate and subject to change based on approval by the Purdue University Board of Trustees. They are provided here for directional context only. Prospective students should consult the Purdue Bursar website or a program specialist for exact current figures before making any financial decisions.

Most residential specialized master's programs at the Daniels School — including the MS Accounting, MSBAIM, MS Finance, MSGSCM, MSHRM, and MS Marketing — have total tuition in the range of approximately $55,000 to $60,000 for the full program. The One-Year MBA is approximately $69,000 in total tuition. These figures are for the tuition itself and do not include living expenses, books, or fees.

Online programs have significantly lower tuition. The Online MBA is approximately $34,944 for the full 48-credit program. The Online MBT is approximately $30,600 for the 36-credit program. Other online MS programs typically fall in the range of $45,000 to $55,000 depending on credit hour count.

Cost of living in the Greater Lafayette area, where Purdue is located, is substantially lower than in major metropolitan areas. Off-campus housing is estimated at approximately $800 to $1,200 per month. This means the total cost of attendance — tuition plus living — is considerably lower at Purdue than at programs located in New York, San Francisco, Boston, or Chicago.

For comparison, specialized master's programs at comparable private business schools frequently cost $90,000 to $130,000 or more for tuition alone. The One-Year MBA at Purdue at approximately $69,000 is priced well below most private peer programs. Purdue is a public Big Ten university, and this public pricing is a genuine structural advantage, not a signal of lower quality.

Merit-based scholarships are automatically considered for all applicants and can reduce the net tuition cost. Award amounts vary by applicant and program. All prospective students should apply by the January priority deadline to maximize scholarship eligibility.
