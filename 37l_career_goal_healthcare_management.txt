CAREER GOAL — HEALTHCARE MANAGEMENT, HEALTHCARE CONSULTING, OR LIFE SCIENCES STRATEGY

Primary recommendation: One-Year MBA.

The One-Year MBA is the strongest fit for professionals targeting healthcare management roles at hospital systems, health insurers, pharmaceutical companies, medical device firms, or healthcare consulting practices. The general management foundation maps directly to the cross-functional leadership that healthcare organizations hire for — operations, strategy, finance, and marketing across a complex regulatory environment.

Eli Lilly is a consistent top Daniels recruiter, and other pharmaceutical and medical device companies recruit regularly from the program. The West Lafayette corridor has meaningful concentration of life sciences employers.

Secondary recommendation: MSGSCM for healthcare supply chain, pharmaceutical operations, and medical device manufacturing operations roles. Healthcare supply chain is a high-demand specialty with above-average compensation, and Purdue's supply chain program has direct pipelines to pharmaceutical and life sciences operations.

Secondary recommendation: MSBAIM for healthcare analytics, payer analytics, or clinical data science roles. Healthcare organizations increasingly hire for data-first roles, and the MSBAIM's combination of technical depth and business literacy is well-suited.

Key framing: Daniels does not offer a healthcare-specific MBA concentration — the healthcare career path is built through coursework choice, career services guidance, and internship selection rather than a dedicated healthcare track. For applicants who require a dedicated healthcare management program, be honest that a specialized MHA or MBA-with-healthcare-concentration may fit better, and direct them to the admissions office to discuss whether the One-Year MBA is the right fit for their specific healthcare path.

For applicants with a clinical background (nurses, physicians, allied health) moving into leadership, the One-Year MBA is designed for exactly this kind of domain-deep professional looking to add business leadership capability.
