ONLINE MS HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT (ONLINE MSHRM) — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The Online MSHRM is the online counterpart to the residential MSHRM. The curriculum and degree are identical; the diploma does not indicate mode of study. The program has the most open admissions profile of any Daniels online program.

GMAT and GRE are not required for this program. There are no minimum score thresholds, and no waiver application is needed — test scores are simply not part of the standard admission decision. Submitting a score is optional and can support scholarship consideration, but it does not gate admission.

Work experience is preferred but not required. Applicants come from a wide mix of backgrounds — new HR professionals, cross-functional professionals moving into HR, and established HR practitioners looking to formalize their credentials. The program is designed to serve all three.

Applicants are accepted from any undergraduate major. No specific background is required.

The Statement of Purpose is the most important differentiating element for this program. The admissions committee weighs clarity of career goals, specificity about how the program addresses a gap, and quality of recommendations from people who can speak to work ethic and potential.

Deadlines are rolling. Scholarship consideration is stronger for earlier applications.

Shared application components are the same as for all programs: transcripts, two recommendations, Statement of Purpose, Personal History Statement, resume, and the $60 application fee. See chunk 24 for shared admissions materials detail.

For international applicants, English language proficiency tests (TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo) are required unless a waiver applies. See chunk 42 for English language test requirements.
