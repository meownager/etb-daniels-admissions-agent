MS ACCOUNTING (MSA) — CAREER OUTCOMES

The MS Accounting program at the Daniels School is a one-year residential program designed primarily for students who want to pursue CPA licensure and careers in public accounting, corporate accounting, or financial analysis. Most states require 150 credit hours for CPA certification — more than a standard bachelor's degree provides — and the MSA is specifically structured to fulfill that requirement while also preparing students for the CPA exam.

Employment outcomes for MSA graduates are strong. 83 percent of graduates find full-time employment within six months of graduation. Graduates with a master's in accounting earn a median salary approximately 17 percent higher than those with only a bachelor's degree. Typical job titles include Staff Accountant, Audit Associate, Tax Associate, Financial Analyst, and Assurance Associate.

The employer relationships for this program are particularly deep. Big 4 firms recruit heavily from Purdue — EY hired 6 Daniels graduates in the most recent cycle, Deloitte hired 5, Crowe hired 5, KPMG hired 4, and PwC hired 4. Regional firms including Forvis Mazars, KSM, BDO, Blue and Co., and Equity Methods also hire consistently from this program. Beyond public accounting, MSA graduates move into corporate accounting departments, internal audit teams, and financial analyst roles at companies across manufacturing, healthcare, and financial services.

The curriculum is directly aligned to CPA exam content areas, which means students are not preparing separately for the exam on top of their coursework — the two are integrated. Class sizes at the Daniels School are smaller than at many peer business programs, and faculty are accessible, which students consistently note as important when preparing for the CPA exam.

The Pathway Program is available for this degree, which means Purdue undergraduates — and students from select partner schools — can streamline directly into the MSA with reduced application requirements, beginning planning as early as their sophomore year.

For a prospect who is an accounting major wondering what to do next, the MSA is typically the clearest and most direct recommendation. The program completes the credential requirement, builds exam readiness, and places graduates directly into the employer pipeline they are aiming for.
