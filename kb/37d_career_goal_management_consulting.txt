CAREER GOAL — MANAGEMENT CONSULTING

Primary recommendation: One-Year MBA.

Firms like A.T. Kearney and other major consulting firms recruit directly from the One-Year MBA. The program is designed for career switchers and provides the broad business foundation that generalist consulting roles require. The STEM designation is also advantageous for consulting firms with analytics-heavy practices.

Secondary recommendations: MBT for candidates with a technology background who want to enter technology consulting specifically. MSBAIM for candidates with strong analytics skills who want to enter data-driven or analytics consulting.

Key framing: consulting firms hire for analytical rigor, structured thinking, and communication skills. The MBA builds all three across business functions. For specialized consulting practices — technology, analytics, supply chain — the relevant MS program can also be a pathway, particularly for candidates who want domain depth rather than generalist positioning.
