ONLINE MS BUSINESS ANALYTICS (ONLINE MSBAIM) — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The Online MS Business Analytics is the online counterpart to the residential MSBAIM. The curriculum and degree are equivalent; the diploma does not indicate mode of study. The program is designed for working professionals who want to build advanced analytics capability without leaving their role.

GMAT or GRE is required for applicants with fewer than three years of professional work experience. For applicants with three or more years of experience, test scores are automatically waived. Applicants with less than three years of experience who want to request a waiver must meet one of three conditions: an accredited graduate degree, a cumulative undergraduate GPA of 3.0 or higher, or demonstrated quantitative skills evidenced through coursework or professional work. If a GMAT is submitted, the minimum score to be eligible is 500.

Work experience is strongly preferred. The program is most effective for applicants who can apply analytical methods directly to a current role — this is both an admissions signal and a pedagogical fit consideration.

The Statement of Purpose should explicitly address quantitative preparation and the specific analytics problem the applicant wants to solve in their current or target role. Generic "I want to work with data" framings are less compelling than a specific use case and a clear articulation of why the program fills a skill gap.

Deadlines are rolling, though earlier applications tend to receive stronger scholarship consideration.

Shared application components are the same as for all programs: transcripts, two recommendations, Statement of Purpose, Personal History Statement, resume, and the $60 application fee. See chunk 24 for shared admissions materials detail.

For international applicants, English language proficiency tests (TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo) are required unless a waiver applies. See chunk 42 for English language test requirements.
