UNDERGRADUATE BUSINESS PROGRAMS — CAREER OUTCOMES

The Mitch Daniels School of Business at Purdue University offers undergraduate majors in Accounting, Finance, Business Analytics and Information Management, Marketing, Supply Chain and Operations Management, General Management, Economics, Quantitative Business Economics, and Integrated Business and Engineering. Every major is built on rigorous data analysis and critical thinking, and students can further customize their path through concentrations, minors, combined degree pathways, and an Innovation and Entrepreneurship Certificate.

Placement results for the Class of 2025 were strong. 94.7 percent of graduates found full-time employment within three months of graduation, compared to 91.8 percent the prior year. The average starting salary was $69,212, and 51 percent of graduates received a signing bonus averaging $7,149. 87 percent of graduates completed at least one business-specific internship before finishing their degree. Top employers hiring Daniels undergraduates include Crowe, Ernst and Young, PwC, Amazon, FORVIS, PepsiCo, Eli Lilly and Company, KPMG, JP Morgan Chase, and Deloitte.

Accounting majors primarily move into public accounting at Big 4 and regional firms, as well as internal audit and corporate finance roles. Finance majors pursue investment banking, corporate finance, financial planning and analysis, and credit roles. Business Analytics and Information Management majors move into data analyst, business intelligence, product analyst, and systems analyst positions at technology and financial services companies. Marketing majors pursue brand management, digital marketing, market research, and advertising roles at consumer goods and technology companies. Supply Chain majors enter logistics, procurement, and operations roles, often at manufacturing and retail companies. The Integrated Business and Engineering program is the most differentiated offering — it prepares students for roles at the intersection of data, operations, and engineering, with specialized tracks in Data Analytics, Financial Engineering, and Operations.

What makes Purdue distinctive at the undergraduate level is the engineering heritage. The IBE program is developed in partnership with Purdue's College of Engineering and is not available at most business schools. The Cornerstone for Business first-year sequence builds communication and critical thinking through a business-centered curriculum. Real-world experiential learning is embedded across all majors through consulting projects, case competitions, the Student Managed Investment Fund, interdisciplinary hackathons, and company-sponsored projects. Motivated undergraduates can also accelerate directly into a master's program through the Pathway Program, beginning as early as their sophomore year.

For undecided students, the key question to ask is whether they are more drawn to working with numbers and data, with people and strategy, or with operations and systems. The answer usually points clearly to one of the major families. For parents, the placement rate and employer names are the most compelling data points to lead with.
