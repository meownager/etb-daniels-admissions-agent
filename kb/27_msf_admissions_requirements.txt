MS FINANCE (MSF) — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The MSF is a 10-month residential program. Applicants typically come from finance, mathematics, economics, or STEM backgrounds. The program is quantitatively rigorous, and the admissions committee looks for evidence of strong analytical foundation in the academic record.

GMAT requirements for the MSF differ from most other Daniels programs. For applicants with a STEM degree and a GPA of 3.0 or higher, the GMAT is waived. For applicants without a STEM degree, a GMAT score is required regardless of GPA. The preferred GMAT score is 550 or above. The average class GMAT score over the past five years has been approximately 630, and the range that the program considers competitive is 600 to 650. On the GRE, competitive scores are in the 312 to 318 range, with an average class score of approximately 320 to 322. Applicants who hold a CFA Level II designation, a CPA, or equivalent professional credential may also apply for a waiver — the admissions committee will consider strong quantitative credentials as an alternative to test scores.

Work experience is not required for the MSF. Most students enter with zero to two years of experience. The program is designed as a post-undergraduate finance credential and early career accelerator.

Deadlines follow a priority structure. The January priority deadline is recommended for maximum scholarship consideration. Additional deadlines in March are available.

The GRE is accepted in lieu of the GMAT. If an applicant has already taken the GRE, those scores may be submitted instead. The admissions committee uses the scores as one component of a holistic review — a strong Statement of Purpose, academic record, and letters of recommendation can offset a test score that is slightly below the preferred range.

For an applicant asking how competitive they are for the MSF: the key indicators are the undergraduate GPA, the quantitative strength of their coursework, and whether they have a STEM degree. An applicant with a finance or economics degree and a 3.2 GPA should submit a GMAT and aim for 600 or above for a competitive application.
