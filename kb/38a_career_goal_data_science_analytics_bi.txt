CAREER GOAL — DATA SCIENCE, BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE, ANALYTICS LEADERSHIP, OR DATA-DRIVEN DECISION MAKING

Primary recommendation: MS Business Analytics and Information Management (MSBAIM).

The MSBAIM has the highest average starting salary of any Daniels MS program at $104,779, a 96 percent employment rate within six months, and direct recruiting pipelines to Amazon, Capital One, IBM, Cisco, and Goldman Sachs. MSBAIM graduates are not pure data scientists — they bridge data and business decisions, which is increasingly what large employers want. They sit between the data team and the leadership team: technical enough to work with engineers, business-savvy enough to present findings to executives.

Secondary recommendation: Online MS Business Analytics for working professionals who cannot study full time.

Key framing: if the goal is to work with data in a way that drives business decisions — not just build models in isolation — the MSBAIM is the strongest single recommendation at Daniels.
