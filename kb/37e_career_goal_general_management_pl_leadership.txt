CAREER GOAL — GENERAL MANAGEMENT, P&L LEADERSHIP, OR CROSS-FUNCTIONAL BUSINESS LEADERSHIP

Primary recommendation: One-Year MBA.

The One-Year MBA is the broadest program at Daniels and provides the full range of business skills — accounting, marketing, financial management, operations, and strategic management — needed for general management roles. It is designed for professionals who want to run a business unit, lead cross-functional teams, or move into roles where they are responsible for outcomes across multiple functions.

Key framing: general management roles require breadth, not just functional depth. The MBA is the right choice when the career goal involves leading across functions rather than going deep in one. The STEM designation and 11-month format make it one of the most time-efficient and credential-complete options available.
