MS HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT (MSHRM) — CAREER OUTCOMES

The MS Human Resource Management program at the Daniels School is a one-year program available in both residential and fully online formats. It is designed for students and professionals who want to build careers in human resources, talent management, organizational development, people operations, and HR leadership. It also suits professionals in adjacent roles — operations, consulting, organizational psychology — who want to make a deliberate move into the people strategy domain.

Employment outcomes are among the strongest of any Daniels master's program. 95 percent of MSHRM graduates find full-time employment within six months of graduation, the second-highest rate of any specialized master's at Daniels. The average starting salary is $86,842, and the highest reported salary reached $125,000.

Typical job titles for MSHRM graduates include HR Generalist, HR Business Partner, Talent Acquisition Specialist, Compensation Analyst, Organizational Development Consultant, and People Operations Manager. Graduates work across technology, financial services, consulting, healthcare, manufacturing, and retail. Representative employers include Russell Reynolds Associates and Alliant Credit Union, along with Fortune 500 human resources departments across multiple industries.

A distinctive resource available to MSHRM students is the Hayes Leadership Coaching Institute at Daniels. This institute provides formal leadership coaching training, which is a meaningful differentiator for students who want to move into organizational development, executive coaching, or HR business partner roles at the senior level. Access to this resource during the program is a genuine advantage over HR programs at schools that do not have a dedicated coaching institute.

The online option is worth highlighting specifically. The MSHRM is available fully online, and the online program delivers the same curriculum and degree as the residential version. For working professionals who cannot relocate to West Lafayette, the online MSHRM provides the same credential, the same outcomes network, and the same career services access.

For a prospect focused on HR or people strategy, the 95 percent employment rate within six months is the most compelling single data point to lead with. For experienced HR professionals considering whether to return for a formal credential, the coaching institute and the strategic framing of the curriculum — rather than administrative HR — is the key differentiator.
