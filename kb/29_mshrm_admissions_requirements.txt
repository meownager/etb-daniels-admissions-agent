MS HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT (MSHRM) — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The MSHRM is open to applicants from any undergraduate background. Students come from business, psychology, organizational behavior, communications, and many other fields. No specific undergraduate major is required.

GMAT and GRE are not required for admission. Test scores are encouraged for scholarship consideration and may strengthen a competitive application. A waiver process is available for applicants who want to formally address test score expectations — contact businessgradadmissions@purdue.edu after beginning the application. Work experience is not required. Students enter from both recent undergraduate study and from early or mid-career positions.

The program is available in both residential and fully online formats. The curriculum and degree are identical for both. The diploma does not indicate the mode of study.

The January priority deadline applies for maximum scholarship consideration. Applications after the priority deadline may still be considered for financial aid but availability is reduced.

The Statement of Purpose is the most important differentiating element of the application. The admissions committee places significant weight on the clarity of the applicant's career goals, the specificity of how the program addresses a gap or accelerates a trajectory, and the strength of recommendations from people who can speak directly to the applicant's work ethic and potential.

For a prospective student uncertain about whether their background qualifies: the MSHRM is designed to be accessible from a wide range of starting points. The most important question to answer in the application is not what you have done before, but what you are trying to do next and why this program is the right path there.
