ONE-YEAR MBA — CAREER OUTCOMES

The One-Year MBA from the Daniels School is an 11-month full-time residential program that is STEM-designated. It is designed for professionals with approximately two to five years of work experience who want to accelerate their careers, pivot into a new industry or function, or move into management. The program is explicitly designed for people from a wide range of backgrounds — including engineering, science, social sciences, and the humanities — not just those with prior business degrees. Students with technical or non-business backgrounds find this program particularly effective as a credentialing accelerator.

The STEM designation carries two significant benefits. For international students on F-1 visas, it extends Optional Practical Training eligibility from 12 months to 36 months, giving graduates up to three years to build a career in the United States before requiring employer sponsorship. For all students, it signals quantitative rigor to employers and is increasingly important in a job market where analytical skills are expected across business functions.

The curriculum covers all core business functions — accounting, marketing, financial management, operations management, and strategic management — with elective courses that allow students to customize toward their career goals. The program is designed to be completed in a single academic year of approximately 11 months, making it one of the most time-efficient full MBA options nationally.

Career outcomes for One-Year MBA graduates span a wide range of functions. Typical job titles include Business Development Manager, Operations Manager, Project Manager, Consultant, Strategy Analyst, Product Manager, and Financial Manager. Representative employers include A.T. Kearney, Ford Motor Company, Symbotic, and major consulting and technology companies. Top career functions include management consulting, operations, strategy, general management, and financial management, across industries including technology, manufacturing, consulting, aerospace, defense, and financial services.

Military officers transitioning to civilian careers are well-represented in this program. The structure, discipline, and leadership experience that military professionals bring translate well into the MBA cohort, and several alumni have gone directly into management consulting and operations leadership from this background.

For a prospect with two to five years of experience who wants to move into management, switch industries, or gain the business credentialing that unlocks the next career level in the shortest possible time, the One-Year MBA is the primary recommendation at Daniels.
