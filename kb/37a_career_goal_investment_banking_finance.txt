CAREER GOAL — INVESTMENT BANKING, CAPITAL MARKETS, OR FINANCIAL ADVISORY

Primary recommendation: MS Finance (MSF).

The MSF places graduates directly into analyst and associate roles at Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Deutsche Bank, and Morgan Stanley. The program is technically rigorous and specifically designed for finance-track careers. It is a 10-month residential program built for students who have identified finance as their specific domain and want depth rather than breadth.

Secondary recommendation: One-Year MBA, for prospects who have broader business interests alongside finance and want the optionality to move toward CFO-track general management over time.

Key framing: the MSF is the right choice when the career goal is clearly and specifically finance. The MBA is the right choice when finance is one of several interests and general management optionality matters.
