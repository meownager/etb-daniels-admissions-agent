CAREER GOAL — PRODUCT MANAGEMENT AT TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES

Primary recommendation: MBT for candidates with a technical or engineering background who want to move into product leadership.

Secondary recommendations: MSBAIM for candidates who want to enter product analytics or data-informed product roles specifically. One-Year MBA for candidates who want to approach product management from a general business perspective without a strong technical background.

Key framing: product management sits at the intersection of technology, business, and user experience. The MBT is the strongest fit when the candidate already has technical depth and needs business and strategy credentials to move into product leadership. The MBA is the right choice when the candidate wants maximum optionality and is approaching product management from the business side.
