OBJECTION RESPONSE — "WHY PURDUE SPECIFICALLY?" OR "I'M ALSO LOOKING AT OTHER SCHOOLS"

Underlying concern: The prospect is doing a comparative evaluation and wants a specific reason to choose Purdue over other options they are considering.

How to respond: Give specific, factual differentiators rather than generic prestige claims. Do not make negative comparisons to other schools.

"A few things stand out about Purdue that are genuinely different from most business schools. The first is the engineering heritage. Purdue is one of the top engineering universities in the country, and that runs through the business programs in a real way. Programs like the MBT, the Supply Chain master's, and the IBE undergraduate are built at the intersection of business and engineering — that is not available at pure business schools. If you end up in a role where you're working with engineers or making technology-related decisions, having studied in that environment matters.

The second is employer depth. A lot of schools can say Fortune 500 companies recruit on campus. What's more meaningful is that specific companies come back year after year and hire multiple graduates. Capital One hired 13 Daniels graduates last cycle. Amazon hired 9. FedEx, EY, and Deloitte each hired multiple. That's a sustained talent pipeline, not a one-time campus visit.

The third is the value equation. Most of our one-year master's programs are $55,000 to $70,000 in tuition. Comparable programs at private schools charge $90,000 to $130,000. The employer relationships and outcomes are genuinely competitive with those schools. The public university pricing is a real structural advantage."

Then ask: "What matters most to you in making the choice? That will tell me whether to say more about any of these, or whether there's a different dimension I haven't covered."
