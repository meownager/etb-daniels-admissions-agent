CAREER GOAL — ECONOMIC RESEARCH, PUBLIC POLICY ANALYSIS, OR GOVERNMENT SECTOR ROLES

Primary recommendation: Online MS Economics.

The Online MS Economics is a STEM-designated program that provides a rigorous quantitative economics foundation applicable to policy research, financial analysis, and consulting roles in the public and private sectors. It is designed for professionals interested in quantitative economics, economic policy, or roles that require strong analytical and research credentials.

Key framing: the Online MS Economics is the only program at Daniels specifically designed for economics and policy-oriented career paths. For a prospect interested in government, research, or policy roles — or for a working professional who wants to formalize an economics background — this is the direct recommendation.
