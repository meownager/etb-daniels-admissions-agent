CAREER GOAL — CAREER SWITCH WITH NO BUSINESS BACKGROUND

Primary recommendation: One-Year MBA.

The One-Year MBA is explicitly designed for this scenario. It welcomes applicants from engineering, science, humanities, social sciences, and other non-business fields. The curriculum builds the full business foundation from the ground up — students are not expected to arrive knowing finance, accounting, or marketing. That is what the program teaches.

Key framing: a non-business background is not a liability for the MBA — it is the typical profile. The program is most valuable when the person brings deep expertise in one domain and wants the business skills to lead across domains. The 11-month format is the fastest path from a non-business background to a full business credential.
