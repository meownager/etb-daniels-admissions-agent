MS MARKETING (MSM) — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The MSM is open to applicants from a wide range of undergraduate backgrounds. Applicants typically have a background in marketing, communications, business, or a related field, but other backgrounds are accepted. No specific undergraduate major is required.

GMAT and GRE are not required for admission. Test scores are encouraged for scholarship consideration and may strengthen a competitive application. A waiver process is available through the admissions office. Work experience is not required. The program is residential only — there is no online version of the MSM.

The January priority deadline maximizes scholarship eligibility. Applications after the priority deadline may still be considered for financial aid based on available funding.

The Statement of Purpose is the most important differentiating element of the application. The admissions committee places significant weight on the clarity of the applicant's career goals, the specificity of how the program addresses a gap or accelerates a trajectory, and the strength of recommendations from people who can speak directly to the applicant's work ethic and potential.

For a prospective student uncertain about whether their background qualifies: the MSM is designed to be accessible from a wide range of starting points. The most important question to answer in the application is not what you have done before, but what you are trying to do next and why this program is the right path there.
