ONLINE MBA — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The Online MBA is designed for working professionals. The admissions profile reflects this — the program prioritizes professional experience and demonstrated potential over standardized test scores.

GMAT and GRE are not required for the Online MBA. For applicants with three or more years of professional work experience, test scores are automatically waived. For applicants with less than three years of experience, submitting a GMAT or GRE voluntarily can strengthen the application, but it is not required for consideration.

Work experience is not formally required for admission but is strongly recommended. The program is most effective and most appropriate for students who have enough professional context to apply the learning immediately. Three or more years of experience is the typical threshold where the program is genuinely valuable, though the admissions committee considers each application holistically.

Deadlines are rolling for the Online MBA. Applications are reviewed as they are submitted, so earlier applications tend to receive more timely scholarship consideration. The admissions deposit of $1,000 is required upon acceptance and enrollment.

The online format delivers the same curriculum and credits as the residential One-Year MBA but on a flexible pacing schedule suited to professionals managing work and study simultaneously. Students in the Online MBA have access to the same career services office, alumni network, and employer relationships as residential students.

For a working professional who is concerned about whether they have time for the program: the Online MBA is structured to be manageable alongside full-time employment. Many students note that the flexibility to apply coursework immediately to their current job makes the learning more effective and the time investment feel more justified. The admissions office can speak to typical workload and scheduling in more detail during a virtual advising appointment.
