ONLINE MS ECONOMICS — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The Online MS Economics is a quantitative program focused on applied economic analysis, policy evaluation, and data-driven decision making. It is designed for working professionals in roles where rigorous economic reasoning is an asset — public sector analysis, consulting, financial services, technology policy, and research functions.

Evidence of mathematical and analytical aptitude is central to the admission decision. The committee looks for strong coursework in mathematics, statistics, economics, or adjacent quantitative fields, and for professional work that demonstrates applied analytical rigor.

GMAT and GRE requirements for this program may differ from other online master's. Prospects should confirm current requirements directly with the admissions office at businessgradadmissions@purdue.edu before assuming scores are not required. The program's quantitative profile means test scores — where submitted — carry more weight than in less quantitative online programs.

Work experience is recommended but not formally required. The program's applied orientation makes it more effective for students who can tie economic reasoning to current work.

The Statement of Purpose should demonstrate quantitative fluency and articulate a specific policy, business, or research question the applicant wants to develop the tools to address.

Deadlines are rolling. Scholarship consideration is stronger for earlier applications.

Shared application components are the same as for all programs: transcripts, two recommendations, Statement of Purpose, Personal History Statement, resume, and the $60 application fee. See chunk 24 for shared admissions materials detail.

For international applicants, English language proficiency tests (TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo) are required unless a waiver applies. See chunk 42 for English language test requirements.
