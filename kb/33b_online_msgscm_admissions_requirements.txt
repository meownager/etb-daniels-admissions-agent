ONLINE MS GLOBAL SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT (ONLINE MSGSCM) — ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

The Online MSGSCM is the online counterpart to the residential MSGSCM. The curriculum and degree are equivalent; the diploma does not indicate mode of study. The program is designed for supply chain, operations, and logistics professionals who want to advance without pausing their career.

GMAT and GRE are not required for this program. Test scores are recommended — not required — for applicants with fewer than three years of relevant work experience to strengthen a thinner profile. Admission is possible without scores.

Work experience of at least 24 months is recommended. The program assumes and builds on a working professional context; students without that context find the material harder to apply.

Quantitative aptitude is important. The admissions committee looks for evidence of this in prior coursework (statistics, operations, engineering, finance) or in professional work (forecasting, inventory analysis, operations optimization). Applicants without either should speak to this explicitly in the Statement of Purpose.

Deadlines are rolling. Scholarship consideration is stronger for earlier applications.

Shared application components are the same as for all programs: transcripts, two recommendations, Statement of Purpose, Personal History Statement, resume, and the $60 application fee. See chunk 24 for shared admissions materials detail.

For international applicants, English language proficiency tests (TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo) are required unless a waiver applies. See chunk 42 for English language test requirements.
