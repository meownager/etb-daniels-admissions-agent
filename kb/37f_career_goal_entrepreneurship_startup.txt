CAREER GOAL — ENTREPRENEURSHIP, LAUNCHING A STARTUP, OR RUNNING A FAMILY BUSINESS

Primary recommendation: One-Year MBA for graduate-level prospects.

The One-Year MBA provides the broadest business foundation and includes elective coursework in entrepreneurship. For someone launching a startup or taking over a family business, the program builds the financial, operational, and strategic skills needed to run a business from the top.

For undergraduate-level prospects: the Integrated Business and Engineering program with the Innovation and Entrepreneurship Certificate is the most relevant undergraduate track. It is available at Purdue and builds technical and business skills together, which is particularly strong preparation for technology-driven startups.

Key framing: entrepreneurship requires the ability to move across all business functions — finance, operations, marketing, people management — often simultaneously. The MBA is the credential that provides that breadth in the shortest time.
