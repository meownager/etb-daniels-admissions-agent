ONLINE MS PROGRAMS — CAREER OUTCOMES OVERVIEW

The Daniels School offers several specialized master's programs fully online, each delivering the same curriculum and degree as the corresponding residential program. Online programs are designed for working professionals who need flexibility. The diploma for each program is identical to the residential version — it does not indicate the mode of study.

Available online MS programs include the MS Business Analytics, MS Economics, MS Global Supply Chain Management, MS Human Resource Management, and the Online Master of Business and Technology. Each program can typically be completed in approximately two years while working full time, with flexible pacing.

The MS Business Analytics online program is designed for professionals with a quantitative background who want to build or formalize data and analytics skills. It is STEM-designated, which extends OPT eligibility for international students to 36 months. The program requires GMAT or GRE for applicants with fewer than three years of work experience, but the test is automatically waived for those with three or more years of experience.

The Online MS Global Supply Chain Management is designed for professionals in logistics, procurement, or operations who want to advance without leaving their role. At least 24 months of professional work experience is recommended. GMAT and GRE are not required. The online program draws on the same employer relationships and curriculum as the residential MSGSCM.

The Online MS Human Resource Management is the most flexible of the online programs in terms of background requirements. No GMAT or GRE is required, and applicants come from a wide range of undergraduate backgrounds. The program suits HR professionals who want to formalize their credentials and advance toward HR business partner or people strategy roles.

The MS Economics online program is designed for professionals interested in quantitative economics, economic policy, financial research, or public sector roles. It is STEM-designated and provides a rigorous quantitative foundation that complements roles in finance, government, and consulting.

For any prospect who mentions being employed and unable to study full time, confirm their functional area of interest and then route them to the appropriate online program from this list, or to the Online MBA if their goals are broader than a single functional specialty.
