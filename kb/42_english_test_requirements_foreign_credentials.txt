ENGLISH TEST REQUIREMENTS AND FOREIGN CREDENTIAL ACCEPTANCE

International applicants whose native language is not English are required to submit an English language proficiency test score as part of their application. The following tests are accepted.

TOEFL Internet-based test: a minimum total score of 77 is required, with minimum scores of 4.5 in each of the four sections — Writing, Speaking, Listening, and Reading. All five minimum thresholds must be met simultaneously, not just the total.

IELTS: a minimum band score of 6.5 overall is required.

Duolingo English Test: a minimum score of 105 is accepted.

All test scores must be no more than 24 months old at the time the application credentials are reviewed. TOEFL scores should be sent to Purdue University. IELTS reports should be sent electronically to Purdue University Office of International Admissions, West Lafayette. Duolingo scores are submitted through the Duolingo platform.

A waiver of the English language requirement is available for applicants who have earned a baccalaureate degree, graduate degree, or professional degree from an accredited institution where English was the sole language of instruction. This waiver covers many degrees earned at universities in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, and other English-instruction countries. Applicants who believe they qualify for this waiver should note this in their application — the admissions office will review and confirm.

Foreign undergraduate degrees are accepted. Purdue reviews international academic credentials regularly and has experience with transcripts from universities across Asia, Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, and Africa. Transcripts in languages other than English must be accompanied by certified English translations. All uploaded transcripts are considered unofficial for review purposes — the university reserves the right to request official transcripts at any point in the admissions process.

For questions about international credential evaluation or English language requirements, applicants should contact the Purdue University Office of International Admissions or reach the Daniels School admissions office at businessgradadmissions@purdue.edu.
