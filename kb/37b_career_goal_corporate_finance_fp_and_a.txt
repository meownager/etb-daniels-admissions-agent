CAREER GOAL — CORPORATE FINANCE, FINANCIAL PLANNING AND ANALYSIS, TREASURY, OR BUDGETING

Primary recommendation: MS Finance (MSF) for prospects who want technical depth in financial analysis, modeling, and corporate treasury functions.

Secondary recommendation: One-Year MBA for prospects who want to combine finance with general management skills and move toward CFO-track or business unit leadership roles over time.

Key framing: if the goal is to be a strong individual contributor in a finance function — building models, running FP&A, managing treasury — the MSF provides the technical depth employers in those roles want. If the goal is to use finance as a foundation for broader leadership, the MBA provides the breadth.
