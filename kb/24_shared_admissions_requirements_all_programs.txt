SHARED ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS — ALL PROGRAMS

The following components are required for every graduate program application at the Daniels School of Business, regardless of which program the applicant is pursuing. Program-specific requirements — GMAT conditions, work experience thresholds, deadlines, and background expectations — are covered in the individual program admissions files.

Two letters of recommendation are required. The admissions committee strongly prefers recommendations from direct supervisors, current faculty, or others who have observed the applicant's work closely and recently. All recommendations must be submitted through the online application portal — paper recommendations are not accepted.

A Statement of Purpose of up to 500 words is required. It should address three questions: what the applicant's professional plans and career goals are, how attending graduate school will help achieve those goals, and what research or scholarly interests are relevant to the program.

A Personal History Statement of up to 500 words is also required. This should complement rather than duplicate the Statement of Purpose. It should describe the applicant's experiences, skills, and abilities as preparation for graduate study, and may include coursework, work and research experience, internships, publications, presentations, and community service.

A current resume or curriculum vitae is required for all applications.

Transcripts are required from every institution of higher education attended. Documents not in English must include certified English translations. If a degree is still in progress, documentation confirming the award must be submitted later.

The application fee is $60. Upon acceptance and enrollment, a non-refundable admissions deposit of $1,000 is required and applied toward the first semester's tuition.

Applications are submitted through Purdue University's graduate admissions portal. The application is not reviewed until all required materials are received. For questions, contact businessgradadmissions@purdue.edu.
