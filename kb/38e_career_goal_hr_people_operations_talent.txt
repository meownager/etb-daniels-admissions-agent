CAREER GOAL — HR, TALENT ACQUISITION, PEOPLE OPERATIONS, OR ORGANIZATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Primary recommendation: MS Human Resource Management (MSHRM).

The MSHRM has a 95 percent employment rate within six months, the second-highest of any Daniels master's program. It includes access to the Hayes Leadership Coaching Institute, which is a genuine differentiator for students targeting organizational development, executive coaching, or HR business partner roles. The program is available in both residential and fully online formats with the same curriculum and degree.

Secondary recommendation: Online MSHRM for working professionals who cannot relocate or leave their current role.

Key framing: the MSHRM is not an administrative HR credential — it is built around people strategy, organizational development, and leadership. For a prospect who wants to move into HR leadership or people strategy rather than transactional HR functions, that distinction matters and should be named directly.
