CAREER GOAL — DIGITAL MARKETING, MARKETING ANALYTICS, OR PERFORMANCE MARKETING

Primary recommendation: MS Marketing (MSM) for candidates who want to build careers in digital marketing with a strong analytics component.

Secondary recommendation: MSBAIM for candidates who want to weight heavily toward the analytics side of marketing — data science, measurement, attribution modeling — rather than the creative or strategy side.

Key framing: the MSM covers digital marketing and analytics within a marketing career framework. The MSBAIM covers analytics more broadly and can lead to marketing analytics roles, but the career destination is more analytics-oriented than marketing-oriented. Ask the prospect whether they see themselves as a marketer who uses data, or an analyst who specializes in marketing — that answer points clearly to one or the other.
