CAREER GOAL — AI STRATEGY, MACHINE LEARNING APPLICATIONS, OR EMERGING TECHNOLOGY AND BUSINESS MODELS

Primary recommendation: Master of Business and Technology (MBT).

The MBT curriculum includes computational business intelligence and strategic management of technology, which directly addresses the profile of someone who wants to apply AI and machine learning to business strategy rather than build the algorithms themselves. The program is STEM-designated.

Key framing: the gap in the market is not for more engineers who understand AI — it is for business leaders who understand what AI can do and can drive decisions about where and how to apply it. The MBT produces that profile. For a prospect with an engineering or computer science background who wants to lead AI strategy rather than execute it technically, the MBT is the clearest recommendation.
