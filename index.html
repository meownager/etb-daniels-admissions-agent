About This Project

This agent was built as part of the ETB (Entrepreneurial Thinking and Behavior) course at Purdue's Daniels School of Business. It uses retrieval-augmented generation over a 77-chunk knowledge base covering 13 graduate programs, 5 applicant personas, and 24 career goals. The voice interface runs on Voiceflow with parallel observability instrumented through LangSmith.

Disclaimer: This is an independent student research project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Purdue University, the Mitch Daniels School of Business, or its admissions office.

Data sources: publicly available program information from the Mitch Daniels School of Business website and admissions materials. Built April 2026.
