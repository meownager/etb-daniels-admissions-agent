CAREER GOAL — PROMOTION OR ADVANCEMENT WITHOUT LEAVING CURRENT JOB

Primary recommendation: Online MBA or the relevant Online MS program, depending on the functional area.

If the goal is broad business advancement or a move into management, the Online MBA is the right direction. If the goal is to deepen expertise in a specific function — analytics, supply chain, HR — route to the corresponding online MS program.

Key framing: the online programs deliver the same curriculum and the same Purdue University degree as the residential programs. The diploma does not indicate online. 45 percent of online graduates moved into higher-responsibility roles, and 77 percent implemented meaningful changes in their organizations while enrolled. The program is designed to be immediately applicable — coursework connects directly to decisions being made in real time on the job.

Ask the prospect which functional area they want to focus on, then route to the appropriate online option.
